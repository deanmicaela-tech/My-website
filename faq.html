Q&A

Questions & answers

Everything you need to know about our pop-up classes and private events.

Pop-up classes

Do I need any pilates experience?

Not at all. Every class is open to all levels, from first-timers to regulars. Your instructor gives easier and harder options for each move, so you can go at your own pace.

What should I wear and bring?

Wear something comfortable you can move in and bring a water bottle. We bring the mats.

Where are the pop-ups held?

Our pop-ups move between rooftops, parks, studios and local shops we love. The exact location for each class is shared when you book and on Instagram.

How do I book a spot?

Tap Reserve on any upcoming pop-up. You'll see the class details and price, and you can send your request right there. We'll email you to confirm your spot.

What if I can't make it?

Plans change, we get it. Email us at info@orvellawellness.com as soon as you can and we'll do our best to help.

Can I join if I'm pregnant or recovering from an injury?

Please check with your doctor before joining, and let your instructor know before class starts so they can suggest modifications.

Private events

What kinds of private events do you host?

Bridal parties and bachelorettes, birthdays, grand openings, baby showers, team and brand events, and anything else you have in mind.

Where can a private event take place?

Almost anywhere with enough flat space for everyone's mat: your home or backyard, a park, a venue or your business. Not sure where to host? We're happy to help you figure it out.

How many guests can you host?

Tell us your guest count when you reach out, and we'll plan the class around your group.

How far ahead should I book?

As early as you can, especially for weekends. Reach out as soon as you have a date in mind.

How much does a private event cost?

Pricing depends on your group size, the location and the length of the class. Send us an inquiry and we'll come back to you with options.

How do I book a private event?

Fill in the contact form with your date, guest count and location, and we'll be in touch to plan the details.

Still have a question?

Send us a note, or email info@orvellawellness.com, and we'll be in touch soon.
